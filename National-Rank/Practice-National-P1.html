Problem 1 — Longest Common Subsequence

The Problem

Write a function called lcs that takes two strings and returns the length of their longest common subsequence. A subsequence does not need to be contiguous — it just needs to appear in the same order in both strings.

Input:
abcde
ace
Output:
3

A brute force recursive solution will time out on large inputs — use dynamic programming with a 2D table. Then read the two strings on the first two input() lines, call your function, and print the length.

💡 Hint: Build a matrix where each cell is the LCS length of two prefixes. When characters match, look diagonally and add 1. When they don't, take the maximum of the cell to the left or above.
