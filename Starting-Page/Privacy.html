Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Who We Are

Upward is a gamified programming learning platform built for students. This privacy policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that data.

Upward was created as an educational project for Infoeducație 2026.

What Data We Collect

Upward collects only the minimum data needed to provide the service:

  • Your display name (provided during signup)
  • Your email address (provided during signup)
  • Your learning progress — XP, rank, completed lessons and practice problems
  • Your AI tutor chat history (stored locally on your device only)
⚠ All data is stored in your browser's localStorage. This means your data never leaves your device and is not stored on any external server.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively to:

  • Display your name and progress in the sidebar
  • Track which lessons and problems you have completed
  • Calculate your current rank and XP
  • Provide context to the AI tutor so it can help you better

We do not sell, share or transmit your personal data to any third party.

AI Features & Third Party Services

Upward uses the Anthropic Claude API to power AI feedback and the tutor chat. When you submit code for evaluation or send a message to the tutor, that content is sent to Anthropic's API to generate a response.

  • Code you submit for evaluation is sent to Anthropic for processing
  • Chat messages are sent to Anthropic for processing
  • Anthropic's own privacy policy applies to this data

No personal information (name, email) is included in API requests.

Cookies & Local Storage

Upward uses browser localStorage (not cookies) to store your data. localStorage is a standard browser feature that keeps data on your device.

  • No tracking cookies are used
  • No advertising cookies are used
  • No third party analytics are used

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the following rights:

  • Right to access — you can view all your data in your Account page
  • Right to rectification — you can change your display name in your Account page at any time
  • Right to erasure — you can delete your account and all associated data from your Account page using the Delete Account button
  • Right to data portability — your data is stored in localStorage and can be accessed directly through your browser's DevTools
To delete all your data immediately: go to Account → Delete Account. This permanently removes all data from your device.

Data Retention

Your data is retained in localStorage until you either delete your account or clear your browser data. We have no control over data once stored on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.
