Lesson 2 — Text Elements

Text is the foundation of any web page. HTML provides specific tags to give different parts of your text the right meaning and importance.

HEADINGS Use <h1> through <h6> for titles. <h1> is the most important (one per page), <h6> is the smallest. Headings also help search engines understand your content.
PARAGRAPHS The <p> tag wraps a block of normal text. Browsers automatically add space between paragraphs.
BOLD AND ITALIC Use <strong> for important text (shows as bold) and <em> for emphasized text (shows as italic). They carry meaning, not just style.
LINE BREAKS <br> forces a line break in the middle of text. <hr> draws a horizontal line to separate sections. Both are self-closing — no end tag.
SEMANTIC MEANING These tags don't just style your text — they describe what the text means. Screen readers, search engines, and other tools rely on this meaning.
Try It Yourself

Headings of different levels:

<h1>Main Title</h1>
<h2>Section Title</h2>
<h3>Subsection</h3>
